What is stored, and where

Entries you add (amount, category, optional note, type, time) are stored in your browser’s IndexedDB on this device. The app’s own files are kept in the browser’s cache so it works offline. Small settings (monthly budget, vibration choice, date of last backup) are kept in the browser’s local storage. Nothing is stored on a VOLTURA server, because this preview has none.

What is not collected

No name, email, phone number, account login, analytics, advertising, cookies or trackers. The app makes no network requests other than loading its own files.

Third parties

Whoever hosts these files (for example GitHub Pages) may keep ordinary server logs, such as your IP address, when the files are fetched. This depends on the host and is outside the app’s control.

Your control

Use “Download backup (JSON)” to export your entries. Delete entries from the list, or clear this site’s data in your browser settings to remove everything. Clearing site data cannot be undone. This preview has no voice input.

Law

India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 and the Information Technology Act 2000 may apply depending on how the app is offered and by whom.
