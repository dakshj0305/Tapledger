# VOLTURA motif system v19.4

## V-cut / controlled flow

The angular V in the VOLTURA mark is the product's single visual signature. A small clipped corner advances the same direction without repeating literal lightning. The motif signals a decision point in a flow: capture, classify, review.

- Signal chartreuse marks the primary action, selected destination and key financial focus. Paper and graphite hold the rest of the interface.
- Use straight rules and restrained corners for records; reserve the clipped V corner for the strongest focal element.
- Dashboard: the net movement field is the primary signal; the signature block carries the V-cut.
- Navigation: the selected destination gets a crisp leading cut. Ledger rows remain quiet and scannable.
- Quick capture: full-screen, amount-first; categories are direct choices and the save control uses the signal color.
- Budgets and analytics: use the same semantic chart palette in light and dark themes. Never encode income/expense by color alone; retain labels and signs.
- Accounts, goals and settings: rely on typography, dividers and clear action hierarchy rather than decorative cards.
- Matte surfaces only. No glow, glass, broad gradients, external fonts or decorative 3D.
- Amounts use tabular numerals. Honor reduced motion, forced colors, visible focus and safe-area insets.
