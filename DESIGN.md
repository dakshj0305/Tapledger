## Brand & Style

This design system embodies an authoritative, editorial fintech aesthetic rooted in quiet luxury and architectural discipline. It merges the gravitas of classical financial publishing with the razor-sharp execution of a modern wealth intelligence terminal.

### Brand Personality & Core Values
- **Sovereign & Authoritative:** Instills unwavering confidence through deliberate structure, measured hierarchy, and academic poise.
- **Architectural & Precise:** Employs systematic layout cadences, tabular alignments, and immaculate hairline delineations to deliver institutional-grade clarity.
- **Understated Luxury:** Evokes wealth management through high-contrast print sensibilities, warm stone backgrounds, and deep, resonant forest emerald tones instead of tech-startup clichés.

### Design Movement & Visual Expression
The aesthetic blends **Modern Editorial Print** with **Precision Instrument Minimalism**:
- **Surfaces:** Tactile, warm mineral backdrops replacing cold, sterile digital whites.
- **Accents:** Calculated bursts of electric chartreuse reserve themselves exclusively for micro-telemetry, live ticks, and active state highlights, ensuring high functional utility without visual exhaustion.
- **Tactility:** Surfaces rely on razor-thin, structural borders and shallow, crisp architectural drop planes over blurred or amorphous skeuomorphism.

## Colors

The palette establishes an intellectual, high-trust atmosphere with purposeful visual accents designed for high-density financial ledgers and performance analytics.

### Functional Palette Structure
- **Primary (`#0A2F22`):** Deep Forest Emerald. Anchor tone for critical UI nodes, primary interaction buttons, key brand banners, and prominent values.
- **Secondary (`#B8EB00`):** Electric Chartreuse. Reserved for high-priority micro-accents, delta markers, live ticker indicators, and focused inputs.
- **Tertiary (`#10B981`):** Precision Emerald. Communicates positive yield, inbound liquidity, and credit states.
- **Negative / Outflow (`#F43F5E`):** Restrained Crimson. Communicates liabilities, drawdowns, outbound capital, and deficit conditions.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#ECEEE9`):** Soft Stone. Establishes the foundational canvas background, mitigating eye fatigue across high-exposure sessions.
- **Surface Elevation (`#F8F9F6`):** Pristine Off-White. Powers card containers, tables, floating sheets, and modals.
- **Structural Lines (`#E2E6DF`):** Mineral border tone providing razor-sharp containment without visual weight.
- **Ink & Typography (`#0C1410`):** Dark Slate. High-contrast, near-black ink delivering strict WCAG 2.2 AA compliance against stone and off-white backdrops.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs classical literary authority with mathematical precision. 

### Font Selection & Roles
- **Editorial Voice (`Newsreader`):** Used across display titles, ledger summary valuations, section openings, and deep-dive analytical headlines. Conveys long-standing institutional prestige.
- **Instrument Precision (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Powers body narrative, tabular displays, balance sheets, interactive micro-labels, and charts. Its geometric proportions preserve legibility across compact viewports.

### Numeric & Data Formatting Rules
- Always apply open-type tabular numbers (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums;` / `tnum`) across account balances, stock prices, delta percentages, and tabular records to avoid horizontal jitter during real-time data streaming.
- Label styles (`label-md`, `label-sm`) default to uppercase formatting for field headers, ticker indicators, and category tabs.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model uses an architectural column framework emphasizing strict alignment, structural cadence, and horizontal balance.

### Grid Principles
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column layout with `2rem` gutters and `3rem` lateral page margins. Content structures around asymmetric modular spans (e.g., 8-column financial charts paired with 4-column portfolio allocations).
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8-column layout with `1.5rem` gutters and `1.5rem` lateral margins. Secondary telemetry panels dock below primary balances.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid layout with `1rem` gutters and `1rem` lateral margins. Ledgers switch to single-column vertical telemetry stacks with horizontal scroll mechanisms for dense data tables.

### Vertical Rhythm
- Inner-card element stacking adheres to `space-sm` (8px) for metadata pairings, `space-md` (16px) for standard field separations, and `space-lg` (24px) for distinct section compartments within panels.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses crisp architectural delineation and tonal surface layering instead of soft diffuse shadows.

### Surface Tiers
- **Tier 0 (Canvas Base):** Hex `#ECEEE9` (Soft Stone). Forms the structural substrate of the viewport.
- **Tier 1 (Panels & Card Modules):** Hex `#F8F9F6` (Pristine Warm Off-White). Sits flush on the canvas, bounded by a 1px solid `#E2E6DF` hairline perimeter border.
- **Tier 2 (Interactive Floating Trays & Menus):** Pure white background (`#FFFFFF`) framed by a 1px `#D3D8CF` perimeter border, underpinned by an architectural contact drop: `0 4px 12px -2px rgba(10, 47, 34, 0.06), 0 2px 4px -1px rgba(10, 47, 34, 0.04)`.
- **Tier 3 (Modals & Command Palettes):** Crisp elevated white layers backed by a deep tinted veil: `rgba(6, 35, 25, 0.45)` with a backdrop blur of `4px`.

## Shapes

The geometry reflects tailored precision, avoiding harsh brutalism while preventing bubbly, playful contours.

### Corner Radius Implementation
- **Base Components (`rounded` / 0.5rem / 8px):** Applied to form inputs, utility buttons, inline badge indicators, and table item hovers.
- **Card Containers (`rounded-lg` / 1rem / 16px):** Standard curvature for intelligence cards, analytical charts, modal frames, and primary stat summaries.
- **Pill Badges (`rounded-full` / 9999px):** Exclusively utilized for status chips, ticker pills, and active filter tabs to contrast against rectangular cards.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#0A2F22` (Deep Forest Emerald), text `#F4F7F5`, corner radius `0.5rem`. Hover shifts to `#062319` with a subtle inset stroke. Focused states reveal a 2px offset ring in `#B8EB00`.
- **Secondary:** Surface `#F8F9F6`, 1px border `#E2E6DF`, text `#0C1410`. Hover transitions background to `#ECEEE9`.
- **Accent (Utility):** Background `#B8EB00`, text `#062319`, weight 600. Reserved for immediate conversion nodes and rapid trading triggers.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- Height: 44px for standard form modules.
- Canvas: Hex `#FFFFFF` with a crisp 1px border in `#E2E6DF` and 8px border radius.
- States: Active focus transitions the border to `#0A2F22` with a 1px chartreuse outer halo. Numerical inputs enforce `tabular-nums` formatting.

### Data Cards & Modules
- Clean stone surfaces (`#F8F9F6`) surrounded by 1px solid hairline borders (`#E2E6DF`).
- Padding: Statically locked to `1.5rem` (`space-lg`).
- Header regions feature display serif typography (`Newsreader`) adjacent to compact uppercase tags (`Plus Jakarta Sans`).

### Data Tables & Ledgers
- Minimalist alternating zebra rows avoided in favor of crisp horizontal 1px dividing lines (`#ECEEE9`).
- Row hover triggers an immediate background fill of `#F4F6F1`.
- Numerical entries align right with strict monospace/tabular figures.

### Status Chips & Badges
- **Positive Yield:** Background `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.12)`, text `#047857`, 1px border `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.2)`.
- **Negative Outflow:** Background `rgba(244, 63, 94, 0.10)`, text `#BE123C`, 1px border `rgba(244, 63, 94, 0.2)`.
- **System Active:** Background `#0A2F22`, text `#B8EB00`, pill-shaped geometry.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Base: 18px squares/circles, border 1.5px `#C4CBC0`, background `#FFFFFF`.
- Checked: Background `#0A2F22`, border `#0A2F22`, displaying a precise `#B8EB00` geometric checkmark.
