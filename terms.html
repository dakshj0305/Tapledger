1. What Voltura does

Voltura is a browser-based personal finance record-keeping tool. It lets you manually record income, expenses and transfers; manage accounts, budgets and savings goals; and view calculations based on records you enter. It is not a bank, payment processor, lender, investment adviser, tax adviser or accounting professional.

2. No bank or payment connection

Voltura does not connect to your bank, retrieve balances, initiate payments or verify transactions with a financial institution. Account balances are calculated from opening balances and entries you record. They may differ from actual bank, wallet or credit-account balances if entries are missing, duplicated or incorrect.

3. Your records and backups

You are responsible for entering accurate data and maintaining a backup. Records are stored in the browser profile on the device you use. Clearing site data, browser profile loss, device failure or browser restrictions may remove records. Export JSON backups regularly and store them somewhere you control. A restored backup is merged with records in the current browser; review the result for duplicates or conflicts.

4. Financial information and calculations

Charts, category totals, budgets, cash-flow calculations, recurring-merchant suggestions and pace estimates are generated from the information you enter. They are informational tools, not personal financial, investment, tax, credit or legal advice. Recurring-payment detection is heuristic and may be wrong. A month-end pace estimate extends a recorded daily average; it is not a prediction or guarantee. Verify important decisions independently.

5. NFC and optional voice features

NFC tags open a URL shortcut on a compatible device. The tag does not contain your ledger or carry out a payment. Compatibility depends on the tag, phone, browser and device settings. Voice entry is optional; when enabled, the browser's speech-recognition service may process microphone audio or speech data outside your device. Read the in-app notice and your browser/provider information before opting in.

6. Acceptable and safe use

Use Voltura only for lawful, personal record-keeping and do not use it to store payment credentials, PINs, passwords, identity documents or other secrets. Do not attempt to interfere with the app, its hosting or another person's data. Do not treat an NFC URL as an authentication credential or security control.

7. Availability and changes

Voltura is a static web application and may be unavailable because of hosting, network, browser, device or software issues. Features may change as the software is maintained. No uptime, data recovery or cross-device synchronization commitment is made by this draft.
