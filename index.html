YOUR DATA, YOUR CALL

Data & privacy

Local-first storage

Transactions, accounts, budgets and goals are stored in this browser using IndexedDB.

No bank connection

Voltura does not request bank credentials or fetch account balances.

Back up periodically

Browser storage can be erased when site data is cleared. Export a JSON backup to keep a copy.

WORKSPACE PREFERENCES

Display & feedback

Preferences are stored on this device.

Optional voice entry

Voice input uses your browser’s speech-recognition service. Depending on the browser and provider, audio or speech data may be processed outside this device. Voice is off until you explicitly opt in.

Voice consent: not granted.

OPTIONAL FEATURE · VOICE INPUT

Before you use voice entry

Voltura does not save an audio recording in its own financial database. When you start voice recognition, your browser’s speech service receives live microphone input and may process audio or speech data outside this device. The browser/vendor, processing location and retention are not controlled or verified by Voltura. Voice input is optional; you can continue using manual and NFC entry without it.
