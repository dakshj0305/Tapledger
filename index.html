PROGRAM YOUR CARD

Write the short expense URL with NFC Tools.

Open NFC Tools → Write → Add a record → URL/URI → paste the expense URL → Write. Scan again to verify it. Do not lock the tag.

Your MIFARE Ultralight EV1 has 48 bytes of user memory. The short URL may fit with NDEF URI compression, but the exact fit depends on NDEF overhead and the tag's current formatting. Trust NFC Tools' result; never force a write or permanently lock the card.
