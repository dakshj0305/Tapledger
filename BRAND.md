# VELLUM — Brand system

**Positioning:** A considered personal finance studio for people who want clarity, not noise.

**Tagline:** Every rupee, in perspective.

## Palette
- Midnight ink `#14243A` — primary structure and navigation
- Deep navy `#1D3049` — elevated surfaces
- Warm parchment `#E9E1D3` — page canvas
- Porcelain `#F8F6F1` — cards and working surfaces
- Forest `#234638` — positive action and financial progress
- Moss `#758A78` — secondary data
- Antique brass `#B69B70` — restrained premium detail
- Clay `#B86D55` — expense emphasis and destructive caution

## Typography
- Display: Cormorant Garamond, with Georgia fallback
- Interface: Manrope, with Arial fallback
- Figures: IBM Plex Mono, with Consolas fallback

## Principles
Quiet confidence; disciplined spacing; hairline borders; subtle depth; strong contrast; no gratuitous gradients, glow, glassmorphism or decorative noise.
